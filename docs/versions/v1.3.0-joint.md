# v1.3.0-joint — joint (replay) detector: tower performance without forgetting D-Fire

**Tag:** `v1.3.0-joint` · **Date:** 2026-09-24 · **Status:** frozen · **Recommended detector**

Stage-3 fine-tune that fixes the catastrophic forgetting of v1.2.0: training continues from the v1.1.0 detector on **D-Fire and lookout-tower data together**, so it learns tower smoke while keeping close-range scenes and the fire class. The context VLM is unchanged from v1.1.0.

## Results

| Detector | Tower smoke mAP50 (960 px) | D-Fire mAP50 (640 px) | D-Fire fire-class mAP50 | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| v1.1.0 (D-Fire only) | 0.198 | **0.787** | yes | `results/detector_tower_stage1.json`, `results/detector_after_yolo11s.json` |
| v1.2.0 (tower only) | **0.728** | 0.106 | 0.000 | `results/detector_tower_stage2.json`, `results/detector_dfire_stage2.json` |
| **v1.3.0 (joint)** | **0.718** | **0.748** | **0.687** | `results/detector_tower_stage3.json`, `results/detector_dfire_stage3.json` |

Joint precision / recall: tower 0.685 / 0.719, D-Fire 0.754 / 0.671. Speed: 7.3 ms at 960 px, 3.4 ms at 640 px.

Keeps ~99% of the tower gain and ~95% of the D-Fire performance: the standard replay remedy for catastrophic forgetting.

## Known issues

- D-Fire mAP50 is 0.04 below the D-Fire-only model; tower mAP50 is 0.01 below the tower-only model.
- VLM caveats from v1.1.0 still apply (teacher-agreement metric; industrial-stack regression).
