Wildfire smoke & fire detector

Stage 1 of Wildfire Edge Sentinel, an edge-first wildfire system that runs on one HP ZGX Nano at a lookout tower and keeps working with no network. YOLO11s fine-tuned jointly on D-Fire photos and HPWREN lookout-tower footage, running in your browser with ONNX Runtime Web. Images never leave your device.

  • mAP50 0.748 D-Fire test
  • mAP50 0.718 tower validation
  • zero-shot YOLO-World baseline 0.002
  • ≈40 ms/frame on the Nano GPU
